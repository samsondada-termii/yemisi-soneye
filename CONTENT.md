## work/logos-and-heart/

All projects Logos & heART

Exhibition identity & experience

Disciplines

Visual identity

Art direction

Exhibition design

Environmental graphics

Print

An identity you can step into.

A visual language that moves from the page into the exhibition. Bold typography, saturated colour and playful framing connect campaign communication, printed material and the physical visitor experience.

01 / Visual language One identity. Many encounters.

Yellow, black and red establish a confident graphic voice. Oversized type and expressive imagery carry it across invitations, programme pages and event announcements.

Panel session / event communication

Performance / campaign application

02 / Print & communication A closer reading.

The printed material brings the exhibition’s story into a more intimate format. Clear headings, contrasting panels and vivid artwork create a rhythm between information and image.

Editorial sequence / invitation, exhibition story and programme

Exhibition leaflet / graphic layout

03 / Exhibition system Colour becomes a place.

Mirrored openings, a stepped pink profile and a large yellow welcome wall turn the entrance into an encounter with the identity. Inside, orange display structures give the artworks a consistent spatial frame.

Large-format welcome graphic / live installation

Entrance system / form, framing and colour Composition study · not to scale

Display system / artwork in context

Installation detail / the physical frame

04 / Live experience Designed to be explored.

The visual system stays present at every scale: a welcome seen across the forecourt, a framed artwork, a label read up close. Together, these elements connect communication with the experience of moving through the exhibition.

Aperture / a view through the environment

Colour / a repeated framing device

Live environment / the exhibition auditorium

## work/egfm/

All projects Who is Like God?

EGFM · Event environment

Disciplines

Event visual system

Large-format graphics

A graphic identity at the scale of a gathering.

A broad, dark stage field gives the event’s central message room to stand out. Tall colour panels punctuate the composition, carrying a vivid visual language across the full width of the live environment.

01 / Visual composition A strong central statement.

The event title becomes the focal point of the stage. Vertical fields of pink, violet and blue break up the dark surround, balancing large-scale typography with colour and light.

02 / Stage system Message, rhythm and scale.

The simplified front composition isolates the relationship visible in the event photograph: a central typographic field, repeated vertical colour panels and a continuous performance zone.

Stage system / graphic composition Composition study · not to scale

03 / In use Read across the room.

The live photograph shows the graphic system operating at audience scale. A generous central field and a limited family of vertical elements give the stage a recognisable visual focus.

## work/singlz-summit/

All projects Singlz Summit

Graphic language & stage environment

2024

Disciplines

Stage design

Environmental graphics

Event visual system

A world within every circle.

A family of circular forms brings vivid landscape imagery into a live environment. Shifts in scale and colour create a playful composition, with a large pink centrepiece anchoring the stage.

01 / Graphic language Images with a shared shape.

Illustrated worlds sit inside a common circular frame. Pink, yellow, violet, cyan and green distinguish the individual scenes while the repeated geometry ties the composition together.

2024 / Campaign communication

Colour, imagery

and a circular frame.

The campaign places bold typography alongside a symbolic illustrated world. On stage, the image-making expands into a family of colourful landscapes, each held within a circular frame.

02 / Stage system From image to environment.

Large circles establish the main visual field; smaller discs fill the spaces between them. The black surround keeps the edges distinct, allowing the graphic forms to read clearly across the room.

Stage composition / presentation view

03 / Live experience A stage for different moments.

The same scenic language frames both musical performance and seated conversation. Its broad composition gives the event a recognisable setting without depending on a single on-stage arrangement.

## work/nlwc/

All projects NLWC

Stage & scenic design

Disciplines

Stage design

Scenic composition

Environmental graphics

A landscape of light and layers.

Warm scenic contours sit beneath a cool, vertically composed image field. The stage uses contrast, repetition and overlapping forms to give a flat backdrop a strong sense of depth.

01 / Stage language Two rhythms, one composition.

Above, staggered vertical panels draw the eye towards the centre. Below, an uninterrupted landscape of layered peaks moves across the stage. Cool blue imagery and warm illuminated edges hold the two systems in balance.

Live environment / front view

02 / Stage system Depth through repetition.

The scenic language is built from related contours placed in layers. Light along the edges separates the forms, while the dark background gives the vertical panels room to breathe.

Stage language / vertical rhythm and layered contours Composition study · not to scale

03 / Live environment A clear frame for the speaker.

Seen from the audience, the composition maintains a strong centre while extending across the width of the stage. Alternate views reveal the relationship between the scenic layers, the image panels and the live setting.

Alternate view / scenic depth

Audience view / the full live setting

Property, presented clearly.

Two campaign applications balance architectural imagery with practical sales information. A consistent blue palette, prominent property names and a clear hierarchy give each development a distinct place within the brand.

Selected applications A consistent visual address.

Fairmont uses a compact information table to organise apartment options. Ajaomews gives greater emphasis to the building and its name. Both retain the same blue-led treatment and recognisable brand placement.
